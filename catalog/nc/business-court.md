# North Carolina — Business Court

Rules, designation template, and eFiling guidance for the North Carolina Business Court (mandatory complex business cases designated under N.C. Gen. Stat. § 7A-45.4).

> **Note — the current Business Court Rules were codified 2 September 2025 and have no resolvable PDF.** The rules page states the current set is codified 2 September 2025 following an order amending Rules 4 and 7 and Appendix 1 (adopted 20 August 2025, effective 2 September 2025); the direct PDF URL for that codification could not be resolved in the research session, so use the rules page. The superseded 16 February 2023 codification is available as a stable full-text PDF.
>
> **Key items:** the **Notice of Designation Template (Appendix 1 to the Business Court Rules)** and the **eFiling Quick-Reference Guide (May 16, 2025)** — the latter documents the fillable Notice of Appearance form for attorneys and Notice of Association for pro se users. Note also that parties file in Business Court's eFlex system **and** must also file with the Clerk of Superior Court in the county of venue (General Rules of Practice 5.0, 5.1).
