# North Carolina — Civil Litigation (NC Administrative Office of the Courts)

AOC civil forms for superior/district court civil practice. Master index fetched: the [NC Judicial Branch Forms index](https://www.nccourts.gov/documents/forms) (1,120 forms at time of fetch). Each PDF below was fetched and its footer form number/revision date read directly.

> **Caveat 1 — the NC AOC publishes NO general civil pleading forms.** There is no AOC complaint, answer, notice of appearance, or note-of-issue form. The AOC set covers **summons, service, judgment enforcement, and statutory special proceedings** only; complaints, answers, motions to dismiss, discovery, and notices of appearance in commercial cases are drafted from scratch. Verified against the AOC's own [Forms Report index](https://www.nccourts.gov/assets/documents/publications/CompleteIndex.pdf) and the [Forms index](https://www.nccourts.gov/documents/forms). Relatedly, NC has **no general civil "motion and order to show cause" form** — the show-cause forms are statute-specific.
>
> **Caveat 2 — nccourts.gov asset filenames are unstable.** Several current forms live at non-obvious paths (AOC-CV-405 is served at `251.pdf`) or require a `VersionId` query string (AOC-CV-382). Always **re-resolve from the form's landing page** before relying on a stored deep link.
>
> **Caveat 3 — discontinued-but-live file.** AOC-COA-81 still downloads even though its [landing page](https://www.nccourts.gov/documents/forms/appeal-information-statement) says it was discontinued January 1, 2019 in favor of electronic Appeal Information Sheets at ncappellatecourts.org. See [appellate-litigation.md](appellate-litigation.md).
>
> **Caveat 4 — the AOC Forms Report used for cross-checking is dated January 1, 2018**, so its revision dates are historical; every revision date below was instead read from the current document or its landing page.
