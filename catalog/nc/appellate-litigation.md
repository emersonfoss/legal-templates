# North Carolina — Appellate Litigation (Court of Appeals and Supreme Court)

Appellate-division materials for the North Carolina Court of Appeals and Supreme Court.

> **⚠️ There is NO "AOC-AP" form series.** Notices of appeal, petitions for discretionary review, petitions for writ of certiorari, and petitions for writ of supersedeas exist **only as Appendix D model formats inside the Rules of Appellate Procedure PDF** — there are no standalone AOC-AP form files. The verified Appendix D source is the codification **codified/effective 1 April 2025**. Appendix D supplies model forms for Notices of Appeal (to the COA from the trial division; to the Supreme Court from a superior court judgment including a death sentence; to the Supreme Court from a COA judgment), Petition for Discretionary Review under N.C.G.S. § 7A-31, Petition for Writ of Certiorari, Petition for Writ of Supersedeas with Motion for Temporary Stay, and Motion for Stay of Execution. Brief formatting requirements likewise come from the rules and appendixes, not a separate cover form. The *current* codification (2 September 2025) had no resolvable direct PDF URL in the research session.
>
> **⚠️ Discontinued-but-live trap: AOC-COA-81.** Do not use it. The landing page states the form was discontinued as of January 1, 2019 and that Appeal Information Sheets must now be completed electronically at ncappellatecourts.org under Appellate Rule 41. The legacy PDF still resolves, which is a trap for catalog users.
