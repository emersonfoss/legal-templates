# North Carolina — Corporate / Entity Filings (NC Secretary of State, Business Registration / Corporations Division)

These are **NC Secretary of State business-registration instruments** — statutory articles and applications filed with the Business Registration Division, not deal documents.

Official index pages fetched and used as source pages: the [Business Registration forms index](https://www.sosnc.gov/forms/by_title/_Business_Registration), the [Business Corporations forms index](https://www.sosnc.gov/forms/by_title/_Business_Registration_Business_Corporations), and the [Limited Liability Companies forms index](https://sosnc.gov/forms/by_title/_Business_Registration_Limited_Liability_Companies).

> **Caveat — the Annual Report has no blank PDF (it is generated online).** There is no static blank annual-report PDF: per the [official FAQ](https://www.sosnc.gov/frequently_asked_questions/by_title/_BRD_FAQ_Annual_Reports) you search the entity, open its profile, click "File an Annual Report," then print a pre-populated PDF to sign and mail — otherwise file online.
