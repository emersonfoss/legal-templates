# North Carolina — Federal Litigation (E.D.N.C., M.D.N.C., W.D.N.C.)

Local civil forms and rules for the three North Carolina federal districts.

> **Caveat 1 — M.D.N.C. has no pro hac vice form.** No pro hac vice or special-appearance form is posted on MDNC's forms page (it lists disclosure, mediator, magistrate-consent, name-search, USM-285, writ, bill of costs, and Fourth Circuit items); handle admission under the [Local Civil Rules](https://www.ncmd.uscourts.gov/sites/ncmd/files/mdnc%20local%20civil%20rules%2020260601.pdf).
>
> **Caveat 2 — in E.D.N.C., the Notice of Special Appearance is the pro hac vice equivalent** for civil matters. EDNC also posts a criminal version, and uses the national AO 458 Notice of Appearance for appearances.
