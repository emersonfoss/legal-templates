# New York — Federal Commercial Litigation (S.D.N.Y. and E.D.N.Y.)

Civil forms, admissions papers, ADR materials, and local rules for the Southern and Eastern Districts of New York. Official index pages fetched: [SDNY Forms](https://nysd.uscourts.gov/forms) (paginated), [EDNY Forms Search](https://www.nyed.uscourts.gov/forms-search), [EDNY Attorney Admissions](https://www.nyed.uscourts.gov/attorney-admissions), [EDNY ADR Forms](https://www.nyed.uscourts.gov/adr-forms).

> **Caveat 1 — SDNY pro hac vice is CM/ECF-only; there is no form.** Since Sept. 1, 2012 SDNY pro hac vice motions **and fees must be filed online via CM/ECF**, a separate motion is required per applicant, and the admitted attorney must then obtain a PACER account and e-file a notice of appearance.
>
> **Caveat 2 — EDNY re-versions the JS-44 often; always re-pull.** EDNY re-versions its civil cover sheet file frequently (an earlier `js44-45.03202026.pdf` also exists) and the legacy path `/sites/default/files/forms/js44-45.pdf` now returns an error. Always re-pull from the current forms page.
>
> **Caveat 3 — protective-order and ESI templates are judge-specific, not district-wide.** In both SDNY and EDNY, confidentiality/protective-order and ESI templates belong to individual judges; always take the assigned magistrate judge's model rather than assuming a district-wide form.
