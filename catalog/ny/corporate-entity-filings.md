# New York — Corporate / Entity Filings (NY Department of State, Division of Corporations)

These are **NY DOS entity-filing instruments** — statutory certificates filed with the Division of Corporations. They are not M&A deal precedent; for negotiated deal documents see [../federal/securities-ma-edgar.md](../federal/securities-ma-edgar.md) and [../premium-reference-sources.md](../premium-reference-sources.md).

Official index pages fetched: [Division of Corporations, State Records and UCC](https://dos.ny.gov/division-corporations-state-records-and-uniform-commercial-code) and the entity-type index [Domestic Business Corporations](https://dos.ny.gov/domestic-business-corporations).

## Curation notes carried over from research

- Only publisher-official domains were used (`dos.ny.gov`, `filing.dos.ny.gov`). No LegalZoom / RocketLawyer / Justia / formfiles / pdfFiller mirrors, even where they ranked highly in search.
- Every DOS form number and revision code above was read out of the fetched PDF itself, not inferred from a page title.
- Where two similar forms exist (LLC vs. business corporation), the row calls out the trap explicitly.
