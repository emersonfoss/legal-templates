# New York — Commercial Litigation (Supreme Court civil, Commercial Division, NYSCEF)

Forms and model materials for **NYSCEF e-filing, the Commercial Division, and Supreme Court civil** practice. Official index pages: statewide [UCS Forms portal](https://www.nycourts.gov/forms/) (live browser screenshot confirmed: "Statewide Forms"), the [NYSCEF Forms index](https://iappscontent.courts.state.ny.us/NYSCEF/live/forms.htm) (fetched), and the [Commercial Division home page](https://ww2.nycourts.gov/courts/comdiv/index.shtml) (archived snapshot fetched).

> **Caveat 1 — there is no statewide summons / notice-of-issue / notice-of-entry form.** UCS publishes no single statewide summons form (templates are judicial-district/county forms), no statewide trial-level Note of Issue / Certificate of Readiness form URL could be confirmed from a fetched official page, and no statewide official Notice of Entry form was confirmed. Pull these from the county Supreme Court civil page for the venue and confirm local clerk practice and local part rules.
>
> **Caveat 2 — the RJI Commercial Division addendum trap.** Filing the RJI (UCS-840) *without* the Commercial Division Addendum (UCS-840C) is the classic Commercial Division assignment error.
>
> **Verification caveat.** `www.nycourts.gov` / `ww2.nycourts.gov` block automated fetching, so rows sourced from those domains show URLs extracted as live `href` values from archived snapshots of the official pages (page existence re-confirmed by live screenshot); their contents were not independently read. Those rows say so.
