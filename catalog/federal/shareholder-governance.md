## Honest gap — general U.S. shareholder agreement

No free, white-shoe-quality **general** U.S. shareholder/stockholders agreement (that is, one not specific to VC/startup financing and broadly usable for closely held or PE-backed corporations) was found. NVCA's free Voting Agreement and ROFR/Co-Sale Agreement are VC-financing-specific, not general shareholder agreements. Practical Law / Westlaw standard documents and ABA / PLI materials are paywalled, **reference-only** resources. Consumer/SMB and SEO-template results are excluded under this catalog's quality bar. For a general U.S. shareholder/stockholders agreement at white-shoe quality, use paid Practical Law / Westlaw access and/or ABA / PLI reference material; there is no free white-shoe equivalent.
