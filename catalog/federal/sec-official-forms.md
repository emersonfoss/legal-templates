# Federal — SEC Official Forms

Official SEC forms and, for the schedules that have no published blank form, the eCFR primary text.

Primary pages (both fetched):
- Official forms index: [SEC Forms Index](https://www.sec.gov/submit-filings/forms-index)
- EDGAR search / filings access hub: [SEC Search Filings](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings) — free public access to EDGAR; also describes the subscription Public Dissemination Service feed
- EDGAR full-text search UI: [EDGAR Full Text Search](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) (HTTP 200, title "EDGAR Full Text Search")
- Bulk/API access documentation: [Accessing EDGAR Data](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/accessing-edgar-data) (HTTP 200)

> **Note — the schedules (14A, 13D, 13G, TO, D) and RW have no PDF form.** No SEC-published blank form exists for Schedule 14A, Schedule 13D/13G, Schedule TO, Form D or RW; the **primary CFR text (eCFR) plus filed examples** are the correct sources. The forms index itself states: "This list does not include Schedules and certain other non-Form Submissions that are included in the Code of Federal Regulations, including Schedule 13D and Schedule 13G," linking to eCFR. Form AW (withdrawal of an amendment) could not be located on the forms index: n.a. eCFR intermittently rate-limits automated requests — 17 CFR 240.14a-101, 240.14d-100, 240.13d-102, 239.500, 230.477 and 229.601 were each successfully fetched and title-confirmed; 17 CFR 240.13d-101 (Schedule 13D) was blocked on retry, so its URL is reported exactly as published in the SEC forms-index HTML rather than as independently fetched text. Filed examples live in [securities-ma-edgar.md](securities-ma-edgar.md).
