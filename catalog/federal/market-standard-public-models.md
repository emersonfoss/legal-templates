# Federal — Market-Standard Public Models (NVCA Model Legal Documents)

Free, market-standard venture-financing and PIPE model documents published by the National Venture Capital Association. (ABA model M&A agreements are premium/copyrighted and live in [../premium-reference-sources.md](../premium-reference-sources.md), not here.)

Official hub (fetched): [NVCA Model Legal Documents](https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/). The hub page lists current version dates: Certificate of Incorporation (Updated October 2025), Stock Purchase Agreement (October 2025), Investors' Rights Agreement (October 2025), Voting Agreement (Updated June 2026), Right of First Refusal and Co-Sale Agreement (Updated April 2026), Management Rights Letter (July 2020), Indemnification Agreement (July 2020), Model Legal Opinion, plus Model PIPE documents and diligence questionnaires. Access: **free download**, no login observed.

> **Caveat for all rows: verify the current posted version and terms before use** — NVCA revises these documents frequently, and the docs contain drafting notes that assume a Delaware C-corp Series A structure. Re-pull from [the hub page](https://nvca.org/model-legal-documents/) at time of use.
